# Product Overview

## AI News Collection System (公司新闻爬取与摘要系统)

A FastAPI-based web application that automates the collection, classification, and summarization of company news articles using AI.

### Core Features

- **Automated News Collection**: Generates search keywords and crawls news from multiple sources
- **AI Classification**: Automatically filters relevant news (product releases, model updates, features) from irrelevant content (interviews, financial reports, recruitment)
- **Two-Phase Workflow**: 
  - Phase 1: Collection and AI pre-filtering
  - Phase 2: Human review and final report generation
- **Batch Processing**: Upload spreadsheets to process multiple companies simultaneously
- **News Browser**: Advanced filtering and search capabilities across all collected news

### Target Use Cases

- Technology company monitoring
- Product release tracking
- Competitive intelligence gathering
- News aggregation and summarization for research teams

### Key Differentiators

- Chinese language optimized (supports both Chinese and English content)
- AI-powered relevance filtering to reduce noise
- Human-in-the-loop workflow for quality control
- Structured data storage with PostgreSQL
- Swiss-style minimalist UI design
